# SmartBazaar – Presentation Outline

## Slide 1 – Title
SmartBazaar Tracker – Know the price before you leave home

## Slide 2 – Problem
Grocery prices in Dhaka change daily and vary between markets. People waste time and money.

## Slide 3 – Solution
Web app with lowest-price view, market comparison, budget planner, community submissions, alerts. Fully bilingual.

## Slide 4 – Demo Flow
Home → Prices → Compare → Trends → Smart Buy → Contribute → Login

## Slide 5 – Tech Stack
HTML/CSS/JS + Node.js/Express + JSON storage

## Slide 6 – Architecture
Single Express server serves frontend + /api routes. Graceful degradation.

## Slide 7 – Key Highlights
Clean sectioned code, transparent recommendation algorithm, role-based registration.

## Slide 8 – Future Work
Real database, JWT, live price collection, admin panel.
